Statistical & Plotting Notes:

• Toggle the "Analysis Data Mode" at the top of the tab to universally include or exclude flagged outliers from all graphs and tables.
• Exclusion Criteria (As per WHO Reference): Data points falling outside the WHO biological plausibility limits are flagged as "out of range" and are excluded when toggled. The limits are: WAZ < -6 or > 5; HAZ < -6 or > 6; WHZ < -5 or > 5.
• Methodologies strictly align with WHO Anthro standard cleaning criteria and ENA for SMART descriptive epidemiology guidelines.
• The Conceptual Density grid mathematically maps Height-for-Age (Horizontal Axis) against Weight-for-Age (Vertical Axis).

Standard Operating Procedures (SoP)

Step 1: Open the web tool by clicking the hyperlink shared through WhatsApp/Email. You must enter all data on the tab named DBI Calculator.

Step 2: Type Doctor or Facility Name.

Step 3: Type the name of the child.

Step 4: Enter Survey date (MM/DD/YYYY format).

Step 5: Enter Birth date (MM/DD/YYYY format).

Step 6: Enter Gender (m or f).

Step 7: Enter Weight (kg).

Step 8: Enter Height (cm) and press Enter or click outside the box.

Guide for Bulk Excel Import

If you have existing data for many children, you can paste it directly into the tool to calculate everything instantly.

  1. Prepare your Excel file: Ensure your data is arranged in exactly these 7 columns in this specific order: Doctor/Facility Name, Name of Child, Survey Date, Birth Date, Gender (m/f), Weight (kg), Height (cm).
  2. Copy the data: Highlight and copy the rows containing your data. Do not copy the header row. (You can paste up to 10,000 rows at a time).
  3. Paste into the tool: Go to the DBI Calculator tab and scroll to the bottom. Click inside the white text box under "Bulk Excel Import" and paste your copied data (Ctrl+V).
  4. Import: Click the blue Import Data button. The tool will automatically create the rows and instantly display all calculations and DBI categories.

Automatic Calculations

After data entry, the nutrition status – underweight (WAZ), stunting (HAZ) and wasting (WHZ) is calculated and reflected automatically.

  • Underweight reflects in the Weight for Age Status column.
  • Stunting reflects in the Height for Age Status column.
  • Wasting reflects in the Weight for Height column.
  • DBI category reflects in the final column.

Technical Note: Calculation Assumptions

DBI Classification Limits: DBI classification uses a Z‑score less than −2 to classify low weight-for-age as “underweight”, low length/height-for-age as “stunted”, and low weight-for-length as “wasted”. Similarly a Z‑score above +2 is used to classify weight-for-height as “Overweight/Obesity”.

LMS Method: The computation of percentiles and z-scores for all three indicators uses formulae based on the LMS method given by Cole and Green in 1992, matching WHO Anthro software.

Underweight-Obesity Anomaly: Rarely observed clinically, but formally classified within this tool as Category J (Underweight + Stunting + Obese). If flagged, data requires careful re-verification.

Age Limit: Tool will not function for children > 5 years (>59 months) per WHO 2006 standards.

Minimum Length: No classification for length < 45 cm per WHO standards.
